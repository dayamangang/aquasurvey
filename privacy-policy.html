Official Institutional Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for AquaSurvey Pro

Effective Date: August 18, 2026
Organization: ICAR Research Complex for NEH Region, Umiam, Meghalaya, India

1. Overview

AquaSurvey Pro is a scientific field survey application developed by researchers at the ICAR Research Complex for NEH Region. This privacy policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards information when used on Android devices.

2. Offline-First & Local Data Storage

AquaSurvey Pro is designed with an offline-first architecture:

  • All survey records and photos are stored locally on your device (using the device's internal storage / IndexedDB).
  • The application does not automatically upload, transmit, or sell your survey data, photos, or personal information to any third-party servers.
  • Data is only exported when the user manually initiates an export (CSV, JSON, or Image Download).

3. Permissions Used by the Application

To facilitate scientific fishery field surveys, AquaSurvey Pro requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely for capturing real-time field survey photos and applying geotagged watermarks. Photos remain entirely on your device.
  • Precise Location (GPS) (android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Used to determine the exact latitude, longitude, and altitude of the waterbody/sampling station to stamp coordinates onto survey photos and plot survey points on the offline field map.
  • Storage / Media Access: Used to save exported survey spreadsheets (CSV) and watermarked survey photos to your device's Downloads/Gallery folder.

4. Children's Privacy

AquaSurvey Pro is a professional scientific and educational tool and does not collect any personally identifiable information from children or adults.

5. Data Security

Because all data resides locally on your Android device, the security of your stored surveys is protected by your device's native lock screen and file encryption mechanisms.
